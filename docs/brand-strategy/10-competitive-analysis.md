## Market gaps InsightForge exploits

1. **The "strategy vs. execution" split is a false choice the market has accepted.** Clients
   either buy strategy (consultancy) and then separately, expensively, re-explain it to a dev
   shop who wasn't in the room — or buy execution (agency/dev shop) with no strategic
   filter, building things that shouldn't have been built. InsightForge collapses this by
   design: the same network does both, so nothing gets lost in the handoff, and nothing gets
   built that Discovery didn't validate.
2. **"Discovery" is usually a free loss-leader, which makes it worthless as a filter.** A free
   discovery call from a dev shop is a sales call, not a diagnostic — it's designed to lead to
   "yes, build with us," not to an honest "don't build this." A *priced* Discovery product
   (FORGE Lite/VDOS) is the only version of discovery a client can trust to say no.
3. **Consultancies don't stay accountable through execution; agencies don't have a strategic
   filter before execution.** The gap between them is exactly where projects fail — a
   brilliant strategy handed to an unrelated execution team, or an unvalidated idea executed
   flawlessly. InsightForge's single-network model is the direct answer to that specific
   failure mode, not a generic "we do everything" pitch.
4. **Affiliate/partner economics are opaque industry-wide.** Every agency recommends a hosting
   provider, a marketing platform, a payment processor — almost none disclose what they earn
   from that recommendation. A stated, visible "no hidden commissions" policy is a real,
   checkable differentiator competitors would have to restructure their entire revenue model
   to match.
5. **Small-to-mid businesses are underserved by the McKinsey-tier rigor** because that tier's
   pricing and timelines assume enterprise budgets. FORGE Lite exists specifically to bring a
   compressed, affordable version of that rigor downmarket — a gap neither traditional
   consultancies (too expensive/slow) nor dev shops (no rigor at all) currently fill.
